Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026  |  Effective: October 2026

1. Who We Are

AlexCyberX is a free cybersecurity education platform run by Pavan Kumar from Jhalawar, Rajasthan, India. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the DPDP Rules, 2025, AlexCyberX is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data you share with us. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the rights you have.

Questions or complaints: use our contact form (start your message with "Privacy:"). We reply within 7 days and resolve complaints within 30 days.

2. What We Collect

  • Account: name, email address, username, password (stored only as a hash by our login provider).
  • Profile and progress: bio, XP, level, solved CTF rooms, hint use, chapters viewed, daily login streak.
  • Things you post: community threads, replies, votes and reports; messages you send through the contact form.
  • Tool inputs: what you type into our tools (for example a command in the Mistake Analyzer or a domain in AlexRecon), to give you the result.
  • Usage and security: pages visited, device and browser type, and your IP address, used for security, abuse prevention and basic usage statistics.

We do not collect payment details (the platform is free), precise location, or sensitive data such as religion, caste, health or biometrics.

3. Why We Use It

  • To run your account and show your progress, XP and leaderboard rank
  • To send account emails (sign-up confirmation, password reset, security alerts)
  • To answer your tool requests and AI hints
  • To keep the platform safe (rate limits, blocking attacks, suspending accounts that break the Terms)
  • To understand which pages are useful and improve them

We use your data only for these purposes, with your consent given at sign-up. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use your data for marketing.

4. Who Processes It For Us

These services process data on our behalf, only as needed for their job:

  • Supabase: login and database
  • Render: website hosting
  • Resend: account emails
  • Google Gemini and Groq: AI features (the text you send to an AI tool or hint)
  • Google Fonts: font delivery

Some of these services store data outside India. We share only what each service needs.

5. How Long We Keep It

  • Account, profile and progress: while your account exists
  • Security logs and blocked IPs: only as long as needed to protect the platform
  • Tool inputs: only as long as needed to give you the result and limit abuse

When you delete your account we delete your personal data, except what the law requires us to keep.

6. Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Database access is limited by row-level security rules
  • Automatic logout after 30 minutes of inactivity

If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Rules require. Found a security problem? See Report a Bug.

7. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it
  • Correct or update your data (most of it you can edit yourself on your profile)
  • Ask us to delete your account and data
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (this closes your account)
  • Nominate a person to use these rights for you in case of death or incapacity
  • Complain to us, and if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India

To use any right, send a request through our contact form from your account email.

8. Children (under 18)

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. If you are under 18, you need permission from your parent or legal guardian, and at sign-up you confirm that you are 18+ or have this permission. A parent or guardian can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete their child's data.

For users under 18 we do not do behavioural tracking, profiling for ads, or marketing of any kind.

9. Cookies

We use only the storage needed to keep you logged in and remember settings such as your language. See the Cookie Notice.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will show a notice on the site and email registered users before it takes effect.
