Security

Report a Bug

Found a bug or a security problem on AlexCyberX? Thank you, please tell us.

1. How to Report

Send the details through our contact form and start the message with "Bug report:". Please include:

  • The page or feature (URL) where you found it
  • What you did, step by step, and what happened
  • What you expected to happen
  • For a security issue: why it is a risk, and a screenshot or short proof if you have one

Please do not post security issues publicly until we have fixed them.

2. What We Promise

  • We reply within 7 days and keep you updated until it is fixed
  • We will not take legal action against anyone who reports in good faith and follows the rules on this page
  • With your permission, we will thank you by name on the platform

3. In Scope

  • The AlexCyberX website and its APIs
  • Login, accounts, profiles, XP and leaderboard
  • Our tools (Mistake Analyzer, AlexRecon, AlexUtils, AlexTrace)

The CTF labs are built to be hacked: finding the intended flag is the challenge, not a bug. If you find a way to break a lab for other players or to reach the real platform from a lab, that is a bug, please report it.

4. Please Don't

  • Access, change or delete other users' data (use your own test accounts)
  • Slow down or take down the site (no DoS, no flooding, no heavy automated scanning)
  • Send spam or try to trick our users or team (social engineering, phishing)
  • Test the systems of our hosting or other service providers

5. The Law, in Short

Testing within these rules is welcome. Attacking any system without the owner's permission, including other parts of this platform or other websites, is a crime in India under the Information Technology Act, 2000 (sections 43 and 66) and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023. Practise only on our CTF labs, your own systems, or targets that allow it.
